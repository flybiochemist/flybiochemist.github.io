Astrocytic ACSBG1 depletion improves lipid–cytokine signaling and attenuates α-synuclein pathology in a Parkinson's disease mouse model

YoungDoo Kim*, Bhupesh Vaidya*, Jiyoen Kim, Sara Bitar, Femil Joseph Shajan, Ajay Kumar Verma, Hari Krishna Yalamanchili, Shubham Singh, Huda Y. Zoghbi. *Equal contribution.

Key findings

  1. ACSBG1 acts non-cell-autonomously on neuronal α-synuclein.
  2. Two parallel mediators, IL-6 / RANTES / MIP-3α and sphingosine d20:1, are each sufficient on their own.
  3. The effect requires ACSBG1 enzymatic activity.
  4. Acsbg1 deletion in Thy1-α-Syn mice reduces astrogliosis and α-Syn, preserves PSD95, and rescues motor behavior.
  5. ACSBG1 expression is restricted to brain, adrenal gland, ovary, and testis, which makes it an attractive target in Parkinson's disease.

The full study is described on the project page and in the bioRxiv preprint.
