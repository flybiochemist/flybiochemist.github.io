I am a Senior Researcher at the Research Institute of Basic Sciences, College of Natural Sciences, Seoul National University. I study how Parkinson's disease begins and progresses, using the *Snca*<sup>G51D/G51D</sup> knock-in mouse model that I built as a postdoctoral associate in the [Zoghbi Lab](https://www.bcm.edu/research/labs/huda-zoghbi) at Baylor College of Medicine.

My current work asks whether the disease can be reversed by helping cells clear α-Synuclein. S5b holds the proteasome, the cell's protein-degrading machine, in check. By removing or inhibiting S5b in G51D mice and in cell models, I test whether a more active proteasome clears α-Synuclein aggregates, protects dopaminergic neurons, and restores function across the whole course of the disease, from the early loss of smell and gut motility to the later motor decline.
