I am a Postdoctoral Associate in the [Zoghbi Lab](https://www.bcm.edu/research/labs/huda-zoghbi) at Baylor College of Medicine, working under the mentorship of Dr. Huda Zoghbi. My research focuses on understanding the mechanisms underlying Parkinson's disease using the *Snca*<sup>G51D/G51D</sup> knock-in mouse model, with a particular emphasis on neuron-glia interactions mediated through metabolite exchanges.

## Current projects

**[G51D knock-in mice](/projects/g51d-knock-in-mice)** — A mouse carrying a Parkinson's-causing *SNCA* mutation at its own locus, rather than overexpressing α-Synuclein. The mice reproduce the order in which human symptoms appear: pathology in the olfactory bulb and gut at 3 months, impaired smell and gut transit at 6, motor deficits only at 9. That gap is a window for testing therapies before irreversible neuron loss.

**[ACSBG1-mediated astrocyte–neuron crosstalk](/projects/acsbg1-astrocyte-neuron-crosstalk)** — How a lipid enzyme expressed almost exclusively in astrocytes changes α-Synuclein inside neurons. Reactive astrocytes release specific cytokines and long-chain sphingosines that drive neuronal α-Synuclein phosphorylation; deleting *Acsbg1* interrupts that signalling and rescues a Parkinson's mouse.
