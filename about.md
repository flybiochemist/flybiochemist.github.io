I am a Senior Researcher at the Research Institute of Basic Sciences, College of Natural Sciences, Seoul National University. My research addresses the mechanisms by which Parkinson's disease begins and progresses. My principal tool is the *Snca*<sup>G51D/G51D</sup> knock-in mouse model, which I generated as a postdoctoral associate in the [Zoghbi Lab](https://www.bcm.edu/research/labs/huda-zoghbi) at Baylor College of Medicine. The model expresses pathogenic α-Synuclein from the endogenous locus and reproduces the progression seen in patients, in which olfactory and gastrointestinal dysfunction precede motor symptoms.

I am currently investigating whether α-Synuclein pathology can be reversed by modulating proteostasis. Specifically, I delete or inhibit S5b (PSMD5), a negative regulator of proteasome activity, in G51D mice and in cell models to activate the ubiquitin–proteasome system. Using behavioral, biochemical, and histological analyses, I then test whether this clears α-Synuclein aggregates, protects dopaminergic neurons, and restores phenotypes across the full course of the disease, from prodromal deficits in olfaction and gut motility to the later decline in motor function.
