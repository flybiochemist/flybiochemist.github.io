I will go ahead and say it: the metabolite drawing the most attention lately in Alzheimer's and Parkinson's is the lipid metabolite.

Today's newly identified genes with a strong likelihood of causing Alzheimer's, from a disease-genome association study, are said to relate to brain volume, autophagy (the cell's self-eating cleanup mechanism), and lipid metabolites. ApoE has been famous forever, ApoJ and CLU before that, and this paper pulls out plenty of new ones too.

Molecular Neurodegeneration, a journal on the rise in this space, has opened a call for papers on a dedicated session — a sign that neurodegeneration research tied to lipid metabolism genes is worth watching, and doing.

And this one study is also a good illustration of how much effort, direct and indirect (supplying the source data others reprocess), goes into a single paper.
