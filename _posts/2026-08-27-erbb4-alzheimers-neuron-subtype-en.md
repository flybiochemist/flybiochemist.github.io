Headlines like this are usually clickbait, but this one is a genuinely interesting story, good enough to land in Nature.

It is a paper that carefully pins down a key gene for which molecular changes seen in Alzheimer's are tied to which neuron subtype. ErbB4 already has a substantial body of compound work behind it from cancer research, which makes it a much friendlier starting point for drug development than beginning from zero. (Crossing the blood-brain barrier and delivering a drug specifically to excitatory neurons remains a genuinely hard problem, of course.)

As findings like this accumulate, I hope Korea sees more full cycles of this kind of leading research too — starting from first discovery all the way through to identifying a drug candidate.
