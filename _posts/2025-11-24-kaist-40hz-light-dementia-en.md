Ever since Li-Huei Tsai's group at MIT published in Nature in 2016 that 40Hz light, sound, or the combination could improve dementia, the neurodegeneration field has been split into camps over whether it reproduces or not.

This new work out of KAIST joins that fight. Of red, blue and green, red is reportedly the most effective. Mice lack red cone cells, so their visual perception of red should be nowhere near as good as a human's (which is exactly why people work under red light to minimize its effect on mice) — I'm curious how it would even act through that route.
